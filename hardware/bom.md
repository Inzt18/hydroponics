# Bill of materials (starter)

## Electronics
- 1× ESP32-CAM (AI Thinker) + FTDI/USB-TTL programmer
- 1× ESP32 DevKit (ESP32-WROOM-32)
- 1× 5 V relay module (opto-isolated preferred)
- 1× DC water pump (matched to your system, often 12 V)
- Jumper wires, breadboard (lab) or screw terminals (field)
- Optional: status LED + 220 Ω resistor

## Solar / power
- 1× 10 W solar panel (6 V or 12 V)
- 1× charge controller
- 1× battery pack (18650 holder or LiFePO4)
- 1× buck converter to 5 V
- Fuse holder + appropriate fuse
- DC jack / XT60 as preferred

## Fluid
- Nutrient concentrate (crop-appropriate)
- Premix tank + tubing + check valve
- Drip emitters or flood tray fittings

## Tools
- Multimeter
- Soldering iron
- Arduino IDE or PlatformIO
- Serial monitor

## Estimated learner cost
Roughly **$60–120 USD** depending on pump/solar choices and local pricing.
